\chapter{Empirical Evaluation and Core Benchmark Results}
\label{chap:experiments}

This chapter details the empirical evaluation of our causal framework on the UCI ID 697 benchmark. Section \ref{sec:exp_setup} establishes the experimental setup, cohort definitions, 5-fold cross-validation splits, and dual-criterion metrics. Section \ref{sec:exp_predictive} analyzes Table A, demonstrating that neural parameterization systematically enhances held-out predictive accuracy and probability calibration. Section \ref{sec:exp_causal_interventions} examines Table B, isolating the causal ATE and proving that over $31.8\%$ of apparent observational scholarship correlation is spurious confounding. Section \ref{sec:exp_refutations} presents Table C, validating the DoWhy refutation suite and illustrating the Pareto trade-off geometry. Finally, Section \ref{sec:exp_ablations} details the ablation diagnostics, highlighting the indispensability of the Temporal Forbidden Mask.
