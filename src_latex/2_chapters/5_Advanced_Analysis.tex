\chapter{Advanced Causal Extensions and Policy Optimization}
\label{chap:advanced_analysis}

This chapter investigates advanced causal extensions and operationalizes the empirical findings for institutional policy design. Section \ref{sec:adv_alternative_outcomes} evaluates continuous GPA, credit velocity, and full retention estimands. Section \ref{sec:adv_mediation} decomposes the total causal effect into natural direct and indirect mediation pathways. Section \ref{sec:adv_heterogeneous_cate} evaluates heterogeneous treatment effects (CATE) across vulnerable demographic profiles. Section \ref{sec:adv_sensitivity} conducts the Cinelli-Hazlett sensitivity audit against unobserved confounders. Section \ref{sec:adv_policy_learning} solves the budget-constrained policy allocation problem, showing a net gain of $+164$ annual graduates at zero added fiscal cost. Finally, Section \ref{sec:adv_kaggle_transfer} validates out-of-distribution causal invariance across 76,518 instances from the Kaggle S4E6 benchmark.
