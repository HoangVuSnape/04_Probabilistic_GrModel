\chapter{Theoretical Foundations and Literature Review}
\label{chap:background}

This chapter establishes the formal mathematical foundations and reviews the relevant literature underlying this research. Section \ref{sec:bg_pgm} reviews Probabilistic Graphical Models, Bayesian Network factorizations, d-separation, and the parameter explosion dilemma of discrete CPTs. Section \ref{sec:bg_scm} formalizes Judea Pearl's Structural Causal Models, the Three-Layer Causal Hierarchy, graph surgery, and the Backdoor Criterion. Section \ref{sec:bg_causal_discovery} provides a comprehensive survey of DAG structure learning algorithms (PC, FCI, Hill Climb, GES, NOTEARS) and details continuous discovery with temporal forbidden masks. Section \ref{sec:bg_estimators} presents a survey of parameter learning paradigms (MLE, Bayesian BDeu, EM, Neural CPDs), semi-parametric causal estimators (AIPW, Causal Forests), and Cinelli-Hazlett sensitivity algebra. Finally, Section \ref{sec:bg_related_work} surveys the educational data mining literature, critiques black-box correlational ML approaches, and delineates the specific methodological void addressed by our proposed hybrid framework.
