\chapter{Methodology and System Architecture}
\label{chap:methods}

This chapter details the methodological architecture of our proposed causal framework. Section \ref{sec:meth_domain_dag} formalizes the 7-node Primary Domain Causal DAG, establishes the four-pillar DAG validity constraints, and proves backdoor admissibility. Section \ref{sec:meth_factorial_matrix} specifies the $2 \times 2$ factorial model space ($B_0, H_1, H_2, H_3$). Section \ref{sec:meth_neural_cpd} details the PyTorch Deep Neural CPD architecture and the Temporal Forbidden Mask. Section \ref{sec:meth_tensor_engine} derives the vectorized $\mathcal{O}(1)$ tensor interventional query engine. Finally, Section \ref{sec:meth_refutations} formalizes the three-tier DoWhy adversarial refutation protocol and Cinelli-Hazlett sensitivity algebra.
