\section{Cinelli-Hazlett Sensitivity Contours for Omitted Variable Bias}
\label{sec:adv_sensitivity}

To stress-test conditional exchangeability against unobserved confounding, we apply the sensitivity algebra of \textcite{cinelli2020making}. Table \ref{tab:cinelli_benchmarks} reports partial coefficients of determination ($R^2_{Y \sim Z_j \mid T, \mathbf{Z}_{-j}}$) for observed administrative predictors.

From the OLS fit $Y \sim T + \mathbf{Z}$ ($t = 14.20$, $df = 3623$) and $RV_q = \tfrac12\big(\sqrt{f_q^4 + 4 f_q^2} - f_q^2\big)$ with $f_q = q\,|t|/\sqrt{df}$ \parencite{cinelli2020making}, the Robustness Value is $RV_{q=1} = \mathbf{0.2097\ (20.97\%)}$, and $RV_{q=1,\alpha=0.05} = 0.1837$. To nullify the scholarship effect ($\hat{\tau} \to 0$), an unobserved confounder $U$ would need to explain at least $20.97\%$ of residual treatment variance \emph{and} $20.97\%$ of residual graduation variance. The strongest observed covariate ($Z_5$) explains $10.41\%$ of residual outcome variance, so the conclusion is robust to confounders weaker than $Z_5$ but not to one roughly twice as strong.
