\chapter{Conclusions, Limitations, and Future Directions}
\label{chap:conclusion}

This concluding chapter synthesizes the core intellectual and practical contributions of this research monograph. Section \ref{sec:concl_summary} reviews the systematic resolution of research questions and hypotheses. Section \ref{sec:concl_recommendations} articulates actionable institutional policy directives for university administrators. Section \ref{sec:concl_limitations} provides a frank appraisal of scientific limitations and threats to validity. Finally, Section \ref{sec:concl_future} maps out promising technical frontiers spanning continuous-time dynamic SCMs, network interference modeling, and sequential reinforcement learning.
